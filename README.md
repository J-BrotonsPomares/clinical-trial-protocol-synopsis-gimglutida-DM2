## 2. Scientific Rationale and Dual Incretin Mechanism

Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM) is a chronic metabolic disorder driven by peripheral insulin resistance, progressive pancreatic β-cell exhaustion, and frequently associated obesity that compounds cardiovascular risk. Despite existing therapies, many patients fail to achieve optimal glycemic targets, leaving a significant unmet medical need.

* **Dual GLP-1 / GIP Mechanism of Action:** Gimglutida is a synthetic peptide acting as a dual agonist of two incretin hormones: **GLP-1** (which stimulates glucose-dependent insulin secretion, delays gastric emptying, and promotes central satiety) and **GIP** (glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide, which synergistically potentiates GLP-1 signaling to markedly enhance insulin release).
* **Sustained-Release Dosing & Adherence:** Unlike currently marketed therapies requiring daily or weekly injections—which contribute to treatment fatigue—Gimglutida provides sustained release via **monthly subcutaneous (SC) administration** (every 28 days $\pm 3\text{ days}$), improving treatment adherence and patient quality of life.

## 4. Clinical Operations, Regulatory, and Data Management Framework

The protocol synopsis establishes core operational, ethical, and safety controls across the trial lifecycle:

* **Good Clinical Practice & Ethics Approvals:** Executed in strict compliance with **ICH-GCP** guidelines and the **Declaration of Helsinki**. No study procedure may commence prior to formal approval of the protocol and Informed Consent Form (ICF) by the Ethics Committee (**CEIm**) and Competent Regulatory Authority.
* **Risk-Based Monitoring (RBM):** Integrates on-site monitoring visits with remote and centralized data review, prioritizing **100% Source Data Verification (SDV)** across critical parameters: inclusion/exclusion eligibility criteria, primary endpoint (**HbA1c**), body weight, and **Serious Adverse Events (SAEs)**.
* **Clinical Data Management:** Pseudoanonymized subject data are captured in a validated Electronic Case Report Form (**eCRF**) compliant with **FDA** and **EMA** standards, enforcing a strict **5-business-day** source-to-eCRF data entry timeline after each participant visit.
* **Pharmacovigilance & Expedited Safety Reporting:** Investigators must report any **SAE** to the Sponsor within a strict **24-hour** deadline from awareness. Suspected Unexpected Serious Adverse Reactions (**SUSARs**) are submitted expeditiously to **EudraVigilance** and involved Ethics Committees (**CEIm**) within statutory timelines (**7 or 15 days** depending on fatality/life-threatening severity).
